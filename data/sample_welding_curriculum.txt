PROGRAM: Welding and Metal Fabrication Technology (Grades 10-12)

PROGRAM OVERVIEW
Students develop entry-level welding and metal fabrication skills
through a combination of classroom instruction and hands-on shop
practice. The program emphasizes safe work habits, blueprint literacy,
and proficiency in the major arc welding processes used in
manufacturing, construction, and repair industries.

COURSE SEQUENCE

WELDING I - Introduction to Welding and Shop Safety (Grade 10)
- Shop safety procedures, personal protective equipment (PPE),
  fire prevention, ventilation and fume control
- OSHA 10-Hour General Industry awareness training
- Introduction to oxy-fuel cutting and torch operation
- Introduction to shielded metal arc welding (SMAW / stick welding)
  in the flat and horizontal positions
- Basic weld joints: butt, lap, tee, and corner joints
- Hand and power tool use: grinders, drills, band saw, shear
- Material preparation, cleaning, and fit-up fundamentals

WELDING II - Arc Welding Processes and Blueprint Reading (Grade 11)
- Gas metal arc welding (GMAW / MIG) on carbon steel in all positions
- Gas tungsten arc welding (GTAW / TIG) fundamentals on steel and
  aluminum
- Blueprint reading and welding drawing interpretation
- AWS weld symbols and joint geometry terminology
- Measuring and layout: tape measure, combination square, calipers
- Shop mathematics: fractions, decimals, angles, and tolerances
- Introduction to metallurgy: carbon steel, stainless steel,
  aluminum, and heat-affected zone basics
- Plasma cutting and basic fabrication techniques

WELDING III - Fabrication, Quality, and Career Preparation (Grade 12)
- Flux-cored arc welding (FCAW) and out-of-position welding
- Fabrication project: design, layout, cut, fit, and weld a
  student-designed weldment to specification
- Visual weld inspection, defect identification, and weld quality
  standards
- Equipment setup, maintenance, and troubleshooting of welding
  power sources and wire feeders
- Team-based fabrication projects emphasizing teamwork and
  communication
- Resume development, interview skills, and workplace
  professionalism
- Optional work-based learning placement with a regional employer
- Opportunity to attempt an AWS welder performance qualification
  test (program goal; certification not guaranteed)

EMBEDDED SKILLS AND COMPETENCIES
- Welding safety and regulatory awareness (PPE, hot work, OSHA)
- Weld quality inspection and workmanship standards
- Troubleshooting equipment and process problems
- Reading and interpreting technical drawings and weld symbols
- Precision measurement and applied shop mathematics
- Collaboration on multi-student fabrication projects
- Attention to detail, pride in workmanship, and time management

NOTE ON PROGRAM ALIGNMENT
This representative curriculum is loosely aligned with the AWS SENSE
(Schools Excelling through National Skills Education) framework used
by many secondary welding programs. Alignment is illustrative only.
